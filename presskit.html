Descriptions & Synopses

One-Line Elevator Pitch

Phonophobia is a first-person atmospheric psychological survival horror game where sound is both your only guide and your greatest existential threat.

Short Description (For Streamers & Quick Articles)

Traverse oppressive darkness in an abandoned subterranean industrial transit complex where acoustic frequencies dictate survival. Solve tactile mechanical puzzles, restore forgotten power grids, and manipulate electrical equipment while evading anomalous entities that stalk every decibel.

Full Narrative Synopsis

You awaken trapped deep within a decrepit, abandoned transit junction submerged in total blackness. Silence isn't safety here—it is the calm before the frequency spikes. Guided only by acoustic reflections, flickering indicator lights, and erratic machinery, you must restore offline substations and mechanical infrastructure.

Every switch flipped, generator cranked, and step taken reverberates into the darkness, attracting predatory anomalies tuned to sound waves. In Phonophobia, listening carefully is your lifeline, and making a sound could be your last mistake.

Key Features

Acoustic-Driven Survival & Stealth

Sound is both your primary navigation tool and your deadliest adversary. Dynamic audio sensors, acoustic occlusion, and spatial propagation force deliberate, stealthy decision-making.

Tactile Mechanical Puzzles

No simple keycard hunting. Directly interact with tactile circuit breakers, rotating frequency dials, voltage regulators, and manual generator cranks to re-route industrial power.

Atmospheric Godot 4 Visuals

Built on Godot Engine 4 featuring custom volumetric lighting, dynamic shadows, authentic retro 3D grain, and an unsettling analog industrial aesthetic.

Native Multiplatform Performance

Engineered from the ground up for high framerates and responsive input on both Linux (native) and Windows PC systems.

Deep Psychological Tension

A haunting, non-jump-scare-reliant ambient soundscape that builds psychological dread through environmental storytelling and isolated subterranean corridors.

Free Standalone Demo

Experience the complete opening chapter set within the abandoned underground transit terminal ahead of the full commercial release.
