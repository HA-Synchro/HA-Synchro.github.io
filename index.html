Phonophobia

A first-person atmospheric psychological horror adventure where sound functions as both your primary sensory guide and your greatest existential threat. Players traverse dark, decaying industrial complexes, restore tactile power grids, manipulate electrical frequency equipment, and solve physical mechanical puzzles while evading anomalous entities that stalk sound waves.

Acoustic stealth & responsive audio detection mechanics
Tactile mechanical puzzle systems & interactive physics
Dynamic volumetric lighting & psychological disorientation
